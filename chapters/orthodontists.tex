\section*{What an orthodontist does}
An orthodontist is a dental specialist who assesses tooth position, bite, jaw growth, and related function. They may treat crowding, spacing, crossbite, overbite, underbite, impacted teeth, and selected jaw-development problems.

\section*{Assessment}
The orthodontist reviews oral and medical history, examines the teeth and jaw, and may use photographs, X-rays, scans, or impressions. They should discuss whether treatment is needed, the expected benefits and limitations, alternatives, duration, retention, costs, and risks.

\section*{Treatment and prevention}
Treatment may use fixed braces, clear aligners, removable appliances, expanders, or surgery in selected cases. Good brushing with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning around appliances, limiting sugary foods and drinks, and routine dental care reduce the risk of decay and gum disease during treatment.

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Contact the orthodontic team for a broken appliance, persistent severe pain, swelling, bleeding, or signs of infection. Seek emergency help for facial injury with uncontrolled bleeding, rapidly increasing swelling, or difficulty breathing or swallowing.
