\chapter{Healthy bones}

\section*{Overview}
Healthy bones is about everyday choices and habits that affect physical and mental health. Lifestyle factors -- including diet, physical activity, sleep, smoking, alcohol, and social connections -- have a powerful influence on long-term health and on many common conditions.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Tiredness, low mood, or reduced concentration when lifestyle is poor
    \item Weight changes that may be unintended
    \item Poor sleep, low energy, or frequent minor illness
    \item Dropping out of enjoyable activities
    \item Reduced fitness or shortness of breath with everyday tasks
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A diet that is high in processed food, sugar, salt, or alcohol
    \item Physical inactivity
    \item Poor sleep or shift work
    \item Smoking and other substance use
    \item High stress or low social support
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Talking about daily habits, sleep, diet, activity, and mood
    \item Checking weight, blood pressure, and other measures such as blood sugar or cholesterol
    \item Looking for underlying causes of low mood, fatigue, or weight change
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Small, sustainable changes rather than sudden overhaul
    \item A balanced diet with plenty of vegetables, fruit, and whole grains
    \item Regular physical activity, building up to about 150 minutes a week of moderate exercise
    \item Good sleep routines and time for rest and relaxation
    \item Stopping smoking and reducing alcohol
    \item Building connections with family, friends, and community
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Make changes one at a time and set realistic goals
    \item Find activities you enjoy so they are easier to keep up
    \item Plan meals and movement into the week
    \item Ask for support from family, friends, a GP, or a community program
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Before starting a new program if you have a long-term health condition
    \item If you have symptoms such as chest pain, dizziness, or persistent low mood
    \item If you are concerned about your weight, alcohol use, or smoking
    \item If motivation is low and mood or sleep are affected
\end{itemize}
