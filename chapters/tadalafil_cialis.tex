\section*{Definition and Overview}
Tadalafil, sold under the brand name Cialis, is a medicine used mainly to treat erectile dysfunction in men. It belongs to a class of drugs called phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitors, which work by relaxing the blood vessels in the penis so that blood can fill the erectile tissue when a man is sexually stimulated. Unlike its older relative sildenafil (Viagra), tadalafil has a long duration of action, remaining effective for up to about 36 hours, which is why it is sometimes called the "weekend pill". It is also used in lower doses as a once-daily treatment that allows men to be ready for sex at any time without planning, and it has a separate use, at different doses and under a different brand name, in treating the symptoms of an enlarged prostate and, in a specialty setting, pulmonary hypertension.

Tadalafil is a prescription medicine in many countries, which means it should be obtained after a proper medical assessment rather than bought online or from informal sources. Erectile dysfunction is very common, is often a marker of underlying health problems such as diabetes, heart disease, or low testosterone, and is usually treatable. A doctor's review is important not just to prescribe safely but to check for causes that need treatment in their own right, and to make sure the medicine is safe for the individual, since tadalafil interacts dangerously with a common group of heart medicines called nitrates.

This chapter explains what tadalafil does, who it suits, how it is taken, its side effects and cautions, and the situations in which it is genuinely dangerous. It is written as a general reference for patients and students and does not replace the advice of a GP or specialist.

\section*{Epidemiology}
Erectile dysfunction is one of the most common health problems affecting men. In many countries, it is estimated to affect a substantial minority of men over 40, and its frequency rises steeply with age, so that it becomes common in older age groups. It is strongly associated with cardiovascular risk factors including diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, obesity, smoking, and physical inactivity, and it is also linked to psychological factors such as stress, anxiety, and depression, and to the medicines used for some of these conditions. For many men, erectile difficulty is one of the earliest warning signs of narrowing of the arteries, and it can predate a heart attack or stroke by several years.

The use of PDE5 inhibitors has grown enormously since sildenafil was introduced, and tadalafil is now one of the most widely prescribed of the group worldwide. Its popularity reflects its long duration of action and its once-daily option. A notable pattern in recent years is the increasing use of these medicines by younger men without diagnosed erectile dysfunction, sometimes as a "performance enhancer", and the growing availability of counterfeit products sold online. This matters because these drugs are safe only when used correctly, and counterfeit tablets can contain wrong or harmful doses.

\section*{Aetiology and Pathophysiology}
To understand how tadalafil works, it helps to know how an erection normally happens. Sexual stimulation releases nitric oxide from the nerves lining the erectile tissue of the penis. Nitric oxide triggers the production of a messenger chemical called cyclic GMP, which relaxes the smooth muscle of the penile arteries, allowing blood to flow in and fill the spongy erectile tissue, producing an erection. A naturally occurring enzyme, phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5), breaks cyclic GMP down, which is part of how the erection subsides.

Tadalafil blocks PDE5, so cyclic GMP accumulates and the relaxation of the penile vessels is prolonged and enhanced. The critical point is that tadalafil does not create an erection on its own: it requires sexual arousal to trigger the nitric oxide signal in the first place. The drug's long half-life, around seventeen and a half hours, is what gives it its long duration of effect. It works in the same way in the prostate and bladder neck, which is why it also helps urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate, and it relaxes blood vessels in the lungs, which underlies its use in pulmonary hypertension.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Erectile dysfunction from vascular disease:} diabetes, hypertension, and atherosclerosis reduce blood flow and nitric oxide production
    \item \textbf{Psychological factors:} stress, anxiety, depression, and relationship problems can cause or worsen erectile difficulty
    \item \textbf{Hormonal causes:} low testosterone and thyroid disorders can impair erectile function
    \item \textbf{Medication side effects:} some blood pressure medicines, antidepressants, and other drugs contribute
    \item \textbf{Surgical and anatomical causes:} prostate surgery, pelvic injury, and spinal cord problems affect nerves and blood supply
    \item \textbf{Lifestyle factors:} smoking, excess alcohol, inactivity, and poor sleep damage blood vessels and hormone balance
\end{itemize}

\section*{Clinical Features}
This section describes how tadalafil is used in practice and the effects a person can expect, rather than the features of a disease.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Onset of action:} tadalafil begins to work within about 30 to 60 minutes of taking a dose
    \item \textbf{Duration of effect:} effectiveness lasts up to about 36 hours, allowing spontaneity over a weekend
    \item \textbf{Requires sexual stimulation:} an erection occurs only with arousal; the pill is not a sexual "autopilot"
    \item \textbf{Dosing patterns:} taken on demand at 10 or 20 milligrams before sex, or as a daily 5 milligram tablet for readiness at any time
    \item \textbf{Consistency:} many men find several doses are needed to gauge the best response for them
    \item \textbf{Food and alcohol:} unlike sildenafil, tadalafil works reliably with food; heavy alcohol should be avoided because it can cause dizziness
    \item \textbf{Effect on urinary symptoms:} at daily dosing it also improves prostate-related urinary symptoms
    \item \textbf{Lack of effect:} if there is no response at all, an underlying cause such as significant vascular or hormonal disease should be investigated
\end{itemize}

\section*{Differential Diagnosis}
Tadalafil is one option among several, and the choice depends on the cause of erectile dysfunction and the person's circumstances.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Other PDE5 inhibitors:} sildenafil acts for about four to six hours, and vardenafil for a similar shorter window, useful when a longer effect is not needed
    \item \textbf{Testosterone therapy:} appropriate when erectile dysfunction is caused by genuinely low testosterone, and it may be combined with a PDE5 inhibitor
    \item \textbf{Vacuum devices and penile injections:} mechanical and injectable options for men who do not respond to tablets, often with severe vascular disease
    \item \textbf{Penile implants:} a surgical option when all other treatments fail
    \item \textbf{Psychological therapy:} the main treatment when anxiety or depression is driving the problem
    \item \textbf{Treating the underlying condition:} improving diabetes control, blood pressure, and quitting smoking can restore function in some men without tablets
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Men should seek medical advice before starting tadalafil, because erectile dysfunction can signal cardiovascular or hormonal disease and because the medicine is unsafe in certain situations. See a GP if the problem is new, persistent, or worsening, if there are other symptoms such as reduced sex drive or breast tenderness, or if there is a history of heart disease, diabetes, or prostate treatment. Men who buy these medicines informally should still arrange a proper review, as counterfeit products are common.

\textbf{Seek urgent medical care for:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item An erection lasting more than four hours (priapism), which can cause permanent damage and needs emergency treatment
    \item Sudden loss of vision in one eye, or sudden hearing loss, after taking the medicine
    \item Chest pain, light-headedness, or fainting while taking the medicine
    \item Taking tadalafil together with nitrate heart medicines, which can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure
\end{itemize}
Contact your local emergency services if symptoms are severe, sudden, or accompanied by collapse.

\section*{Diagnosis and Investigations}
The medical assessment before prescribing tadalafil focuses on the cause of erectile dysfunction and on safety for the heart.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{History:} onset, duration, and circumstances of the problem, whether it occurs with all partners and situations, and whether morning erections occur
    \item \textbf{Review of medicines and heart disease:} critical for identifying dangerous interactions, especially with nitrates
    \item \textbf{Physical examination:} blood pressure, pulses in the legs, genital examination, and assessment for signs of heart or prostate disease
    \item \textbf{Blood tests:} blood glucose, cholesterol, kidney and liver function, and sometimes testosterone and thyroid tests
    \item \textbf{Cardiovascular risk assessment:} the doctor may estimate overall heart risk, since erectile dysfunction is a marker of vascular disease
    \item \textbf{Specialist referral:} urology assessment if there is a complex cause, previous pelvic surgery, or no response to treatment
\end{itemize}

\section*{Management}
Tadalafil is used successfully by most men, but it must be taken correctly and combined with attention to the underlying health issues.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{On-demand use:} a 10 or 20 milligram tablet taken about 30 to 60 minutes before sex, effective for up to 36 hours
    \item \textbf{Daily low-dose use:} a 5 milligram tablet taken every day, which provides constant readiness and also helps prostate symptoms
    \item \textbf{Addressing risk factors:} improving diabetes control, blood pressure, weight, and fitness improves the response and overall health
    \item \textbf{Smoking cessation and alcohol reduction:} both significantly improve erectile function and vascular health
    \item \textbf{Psychological support:} counselling or treatment of anxiety and depression is often needed alongside the medicine
    \item \textbf{Combination treatment:} tadalafil can be combined with vacuum devices or other therapies in difficult cases
    \item \textbf{Follow-up:} a review to assess response, adjust the dose, and check for side effects is part of responsible prescribing
\end{itemize}

\section*{Complications}
Tadalafil is generally safe, but it does have side effects and a small number of serious complications that users should know.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Headache, flushing, and nasal congestion:} the most common side effects, usually mild and brief
    \item \textbf{Indigestion and back or muscle ache:} typical of tadalafil in particular, sometimes lasting a day or two
    \item \textbf{Dizziness:} can occur, especially with alcohol or in those with low blood pressure
    \item \textbf{Priapism:} a rare but serious prolonged erection that needs emergency treatment to prevent permanent damage
    \item \textbf{Non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy:} a very rare cause of sudden vision loss, more likely in those with certain eye and vascular risk factors
    \item \textbf{Sudden hearing loss:} reported rarely and needs urgent review
    \item \textbf{Dangerous interaction with nitrates:} combining tadalafil with nitrate medicines can cause a severe, life-threatening drop in blood pressure
    \item \textbf{Counterfeit products:} fake tablets may be under-dosed or contain harmful ingredients
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prognosis and Outcomes}
For men with erectile dysfunction, the outlook with treatment is good. The large majority of men who take tadalafil report improved erections and satisfactory sexual function, and most continue the medicine long term. Because erectile dysfunction is so often a sign of vascular disease, the most valuable outcome of seeking treatment is often not the prescription itself but the health check that comes with it: many men are diagnosed with diabetes, high blood pressure, or high cholesterol for the first time, and treating these protects the heart as well as restoring sexual function.

In men without underlying disease, and in those who address their risk factors, erectile function can improve substantially, and some men regain adequate function without ongoing medicine. The key predictors of a good outcome are a correct diagnosis, safe prescribing, and a realistic understanding of how the medicine works. With proper use, tadalafil allows most men to resume a satisfying sexual life, and the long duration of action makes it one of the most convenient options available.

\section*{Prevention and Self-Care}
\begin{itemize}
    \item See a GP for a proper assessment before using any erectile dysfunction medicine
    \item Never buy tadalafil online or from unregulated sources, where counterfeit products are common
    \item Do not use tadalafil with nitrate heart medicines, and tell every doctor what you are taking
    \item Keep to a heart-healthy lifestyle with regular activity, a balanced diet, and a healthy weight
    \item Limit alcohol, stop smoking, and protect your sleep, all of which affect erectile function
    \item Take the medicine only as directed, and seek urgent help if an erection lasts more than four hours
    \item Talk openly with your GP about sexual health concerns; it is a normal part of medical care
\end{itemize}
