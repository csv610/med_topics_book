\chapter{Illicit Drug Use}

\section*{Overview}
Illicit Drug Use is a medical condition that affects various body systems. This chapter provides evidence-based information for educational purposes.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Consult a healthcare provider for proper diagnosis
  \item Symptoms vary by individual and condition severity
  \item Keep a symptom diary to track patterns
  \item Report new or worsening symptoms promptly
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
The exact causes of Illicit Drug Use may vary. Research continues to identify genetic, environmental, and lifestyle factors that contribute to this condition.

\section*{Diagnosis}
Diagnosis of Illicit Drug Use typically involves a comprehensive medical evaluation including medical history, physical examination, and appropriate diagnostic testing.

\section*{Treatment}
Treatment approaches for Illicit Drug Use are individualized based on disease severity and patient factors. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for personalized treatment recommendations.

\section*{Prevention}
Prevention strategies for Illicit Drug Use include lifestyle modifications, regular health screenings, and appropriate medical care as recommended by your healthcare provider.

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Seek medical attention for Illicit Drug Use if you experience severe or worsening symptoms, new concerning signs, or if you belong to a high-risk group.
