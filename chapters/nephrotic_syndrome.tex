\chapter{Nephrotic syndrome}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Educational draft; seek professional advice for individual care.}

\section*{Definition and scope}
Nephrotic syndrome is a specific clinical condition requiring assessment of its defining features, likely causes, severity, and impact on function.

\section*{Contributors and risk}
Consider inherited susceptibility, inflammation, infection, injury, immune or metabolic disease, medication effects, and environmental exposures.

\section*{Presentation}
Document onset, progression, severity, associated symptoms, past and family history, medicines, and relevant exposures.

\section*{Assessment}
Use history and examination with targeted tests, imaging, monitoring, or specialist input when indicated.

\section*{Management}
Management may include education, observation, lifestyle measures, medicines, rehabilitation, procedures, or referral.

\section*{When to seek urgent care}
Seek urgent care for sudden severe symptoms, collapse, breathing difficulty, chest pain, new neurological deficit, or rapid deterioration.

\section*{Prevention and follow-up}
Follow the care plan, attend monitoring, reduce modifiable risks, and arrange review for persistent or changing symptoms.

\section*{Sources and limitations}
This concise educational overview should be checked against current local clinical guidance and adapted to the individual.
