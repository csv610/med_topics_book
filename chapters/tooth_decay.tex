\chapter{Tooth decay}

\section*{Overview}
Tooth decay relates to a body system or part of the body. Understanding the normal structure and function of the body helps explain how diseases or injuries affect it, and how treatments work.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Pain, swelling, or tenderness in the affected area
    \item Loss of normal movement, strength, or function
    \item Changes such as redness, warmth, lumps, or colour changes
    \item Numbness, tingling, or unusual sensations
    \item Symptoms that depend on the organ or system involved
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Wear and tear with age
    \item Injury or overuse
    \item Infection or inflammation
    \item Conditions present from birth
    \item Tumours, hormone changes, or problems with blood supply
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item History and physical examination of the relevant area
    \item Blood, urine, or other sample tests
    \item Imaging such as X-ray, ultrasound, CT, or MRI
    \item Specialist tests such as nerve studies,scopes, or function tests
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Self-care and lifestyle changes
    \item Medicines such as pain relief, anti-inflammatories, or targeted drugs
    \item Physiotherapy or other rehabilitation
    \item Procedures or surgery when needed
    \item Ongoing monitoring of long-term conditions
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Stay active, eat a balanced diet, and keep a healthy weight
    \item Avoid smoking and limit alcohol
    \item Use protective equipment at work and during sport
    \item Keep up to date with screening and check-ups
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
See a doctor if symptoms are new, severe, persistent, or interfering with daily life. \textbf{Call 000} for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or sudden weakness
    \item Loss of consciousness, fitting, or sudden severe pain
    \item Heavy bleeding or major injury
\end{itemize}
