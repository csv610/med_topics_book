\chapter{Coping with a terminal illness}

\section*{Overview}
Coping with a terminal illness is a medical topic covered in this reference book. This chapter gives general information in plain English under the standard section headings. For advice specific to you or a family member, speak with a qualified health professional.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Changes in the way the body or mind normally works
    \item Pain, discomfort, or reduced function
    \item Tiredness, mood change, or changes in sleep or appetite
    \item Other changes that have appeared recently or that are getting worse
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Common causes include infections, lifestyle factors, and ageing
    \item Injury or inflammation
    \item Underlying long-term health conditions
    \item Side effects of medicines or treatments
    \item Inherited or genetic factors
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A medical history and discussion of the symptom
    \item A physical examination
    \item Blood, urine, or other samples
    \item Imaging such as X-ray, ultrasound, CT, or MRI
    \item Specialist referral for further assessment
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Self-care and lifestyle change
    \item Medicine to relieve symptoms or treat the cause
    \item Physical therapies, exercise, or rehabilitation
    \item Procedures or surgery when needed
    \item Follow-up to check that the treatment is working
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Maintain a healthy lifestyle with regular activity and a balanced diet
    \item Keep up to date with recommended screening and vaccinations
    \item Avoid smoking, limit alcohol, and use protective equipment as needed
    \item Manage existing health problems and follow treatment plans
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
See a doctor if a symptom is new, persistent, severe, or affecting daily life. \textbf{Call 000} for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or severe bleeding
    \item Sudden weakness, numbness, confusion, or trouble speaking
    \item Loss of consciousness or a fit
    \item Any sudden, severe symptom that worries you
\end{itemize}
