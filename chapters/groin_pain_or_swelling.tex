\chapter{Groin pain or swelling}

\section*{Overview}
Groin pain or swelling is a symptom -- a change you notice in your body -- rather than a disease in itself. Symptoms can arise from many different causes, ranging from minor and self-limiting to serious. The aim of assessment is to work out what is causing the symptom and whether treatment is needed.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The symptom itself, which may be mild, moderate, or severe
    \item Other changes that often go along with it, such as fever, tiredness, or changes in appetite
    \item Triggers or things that make the symptom better or worse
    \item How long the symptom has been present and whether it is getting better or worse
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Common infections such as colds and throat or urinary infections
    \item Inflammation, allergy, or irritation
    \item Injury or overuse
    \item Side effects of medicines
    \item Stress, anxiety, or mood changes
    \item Long-term conditions such as asthma, diabetes, or arthritis
    \item More serious diseases, sometimes needing prompt treatment
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A clear description of the symptom: when it started, how it feels, what brings it on, and what helps
    \item Physical examination
    \item Targeted tests such as blood or urine tests, or imaging such as X-ray or ultrasound
    \item Review of medicines and lifestyle
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Treating the underlying cause once it is found
    \item Symptom relief such as paracetamol for pain or fluids for fever
    \item Rest,adjusted activity, and self-care measures
    \item Specific medicines when indicated, such as antibiotics for a bacterial infection
    \item Follow-up to check that the symptom has settled
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A healthy lifestyle with regular exercise, a balanced diet, and good sleep
    \item Hand washing and recommended vaccinations
    \item Safe lifting, use of protective equipment, and sensible pacing of activity
    \item Not smoking and limiting alcohol
    \item Regular check-ups and managing existing health conditions
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Seek medical care if the symptom is severe, lasts longer than a few days, keeps coming back, or is accompanied by warning signs. \textbf{Call 000} for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or severe abdominal pain
    \item Sudden weakness, numbness, confusion, or trouble speaking
    \item Heavy bleeding or a major injury
    \item A symptom accompanied by collapse, fitting, or loss of consciousness
\end{itemize}
