\section*{Definition and Overview}
Muscle relaxation and stress-relief techniques are strategies that reduce physical tension and help regulate stress responses. They can support wellbeing and symptom management but do not replace assessment of persistent pain or a mental-health condition.

\section*{Techniques}
Options include slow diaphragmatic breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, stretching, mindfulness, guided imagery, yoga, regular physical activity, and a consistent sleep routine. Choose a comfortable technique and practise regularly.

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Seek medical review for new severe pain, weakness, numbness, fever, injury, chest pain, or symptoms that persist. Seek urgent help for difficulty breathing, collapse, or thoughts of self-harm.

\section*{Safe Practice}
Relaxation should not cause sharp pain, dizziness, breath-holding, or worsening anxiety. People with significant injury, pregnancy-related concerns, or chronic disease should ask a clinician about suitable activity.

\section*{Self-Care}
Start gently, use reliable instruction, pace activity, reduce excess caffeine and alcohol, and combine relaxation with practical support for the source of stress. A GP or mental-health professional can help when stress impairs daily functioning.
