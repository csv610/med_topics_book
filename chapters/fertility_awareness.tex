\chapter{Fertility awareness}

\section*{Overview}
Fertility awareness relates to women's reproductive and sexual health, including the menstrual cycle, pregnancy, childbirth, and menopause. Many of these changes are a normal part of life; others may benefit from advice, monitoring, or treatment. Care can be provided by a GP, midwife, obstetrician, or gynaecologist depending on the issue.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Changes in the menstrual cycle, such as heavier, lighter, irregular, or missed periods
    \item Painful periods (dysmenorrhoea) or pain during sex
    \item Unusual vaginal bleeding, including bleeding between periods or after menopause
    \item Vaginal discharge that changes in colour, amount, or smell
    \item Pelvic pain or pressure
    \item Hot flushes, night sweats, and mood changes around menopause
    \item Difficulty becoming pregnant
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Normal hormonal changes across life, including puberty, pregnancy, and menopause
    \item Hormonal conditions such as polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
    \item Structural conditions such as fibroids or endometriosis
    \item Infections of the vagina, cervix, or pelvis
    \item Pelvic floor weakness after childbirth or with age
    \item Stress, weight change, and some medicines
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Medical history, including menstrual and pregnancy history
    \item Pelvic examination
    \item Swabs and tests for infections
    \item Blood tests for hormones, full blood count, and iron
    \item Pelvic ultrasound and other imaging
    \item Smear or cervical screening tests
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lifestyle measures such as a healthy diet, exercise, and stress management
    \item Hormonal treatments such as the contraceptive pill or hormone replacement therapy
    \item Medicines for pain or to treat infection
    \item Specialist treatments for conditions such as endometriosis or fibroids
    \item Pelvic floor physiotherapy for weakness or prolapse
    \item Surgery when other treatments have not helped
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Keep up to date with cervical screening and recommended vaccinations
    \item Maintain a healthy weight and exercise regularly
    \item Use contraception to plan pregnancy and prevent sexually transmitted infections
    \item Take folic acid before and during early pregnancy
    \item Avoid smoking and limit alcohol, especially in pregnancy
    \item Seek pre-pregnancy care if planning a pregnancy
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
See a doctor if menstrual pattern changes, if there is unusual bleeding, persistent pelvic pain, or unusual discharge. \textbf{Call 000} for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Heavy vaginal bleeding that soaks pads quickly, especially in pregnancy
    \item Severe abdominal or pelvic pain
    \item Fever with pelvic pain or discharge
    \item Bleeding or severe pain after miscarriage, abortion, or childbirth
    \item Possible miscarriage, ectopic pregnancy, or other pregnancy emergency
\end{itemize}
