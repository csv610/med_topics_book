\chapter{Type 1 Diabetes}
\index{Type 1 Diabetes}

\section*{Definition and Overview}
Type 1 diabetes (T1D) is an autoimmune disease characterised by T-cell-mediated destruction of pancreatic $\beta$-cells, leading to absolute insulin deficiency. It accounts for ~10\% of all diabetes. Onset is most common in childhood and adolescence (peak 10--14 years) but can occur at any age; adult-onset T1D (LADA -- latent autoimmune diabetes in adults) is increasingly recognised. Without exogenous insulin, T1D is rapidly fatal due to diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). Modern management with insulin therapy, glucose monitoring, and technology enables near-normal life expectancy and quality of life, though the burden of self-management is substantial.

\section*{Epidemiology}
Type 1 diabetes occurs worldwide, with incidence varying substantially between populations and increasing in some regions. It can affect people of any ancestry, although genetic and environmental factors influence risk. Family history increases risk, but most people with type 1 diabetes do not have an affected first-degree relative. Seasonal variation and viral exposures have been studied as possible contributors, but no single trigger explains most cases.

\section*{Aetiology and Pathophysiology}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Autoimmunity:} CD4+ and CD8+ T-cells infiltrate islets (insulitis); autoantibodies against insulin (IAA), GAD65 (GADA), IA-2 (IA-2A), ZnT8 (ZnT8A), tetraspanin-7; appear months to years before clinical onset
    \item \textbf{Genetics:} HLA class II (DR3-DQ2, DR4-DQ8) confers 40--50\% familial risk; >50 non-HLA loci (INS, PTPN22, CTLA4, IL2RA); polygenic risk scores emerging for prediction
    \item \textbf{Environmental triggers:} enteroviruses (coxsackie B), early cow's milk exposure (controversial), vitamin D deficiency, gut microbiome dysbiosis, rapid weight gain/insulin resistance (accelerator hypothesis)
    \item \textbf{Stages (JDRF/ADA/Endocrine Society):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Stage 1: $\ge$2 autoantibodies, normoglycaemia
        \item Stage 2: $\ge$2 autoantibodies, dysglycaemia (IFG/IGT)
        \item Stage 3: symptomatic T1D (hyperglycaemia)
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Honeymoon phase:} partial remission after diagnosis (residual $\beta$-cell function); lower insulin requirements; duration months to years; C-peptide measurable
    \item \textbf{Metabolic consequences:} insulin deficiency $\to$ uncontrolled lipolysis (ketogenesis), proteolysis, gluconeogenesis $\to$ hyperglycaemia, ketonaemia, osmotic diuresis, electrolyte depletion
\end{itemize}

\section*{Clinical Features}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Classic presentation (weeks):} polyuria, polydipsia, nocturia/enuresis, weight loss despite polyphagia, fatigue, blurred vision, recurrent infections (candidiasis, UTIs)
    \item \textbf{Acute onset DKA (medical emergency):} nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, Kussmaul breathing, acetone breath, dehydration, altered consciousness, hypotension; more common in young children (<5 yr) and at diagnosis (~30--40\% in many countries)
    \item \textbf{LADA (adult-onset):} age >30, non-obese, autoantibody positive, initially non-insulin-requiring, slower progression; often misdiagnosed as T2D
    \item \textbf{Associated autoimmunity:} thyroid (Hashimoto's, Graves) 15--30\%, coeliac disease 5--10\%, Addison's disease, vitiligo, pernicious anaemia; screen at diagnosis and periodically
\end{itemize}

\section*{Differential Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Type 2 diabetes:} older, overweight/obese, insulin resistance features (acanthosis, PCOS), negative autoantibodies, measurable C-peptide, family history of T2D
    \item \textbf{Monogenic diabetes (MODY):} autosomal dominant, onset <25, non-insulin-dependent initially, strong multi-generational family history, negative autoantibodies; HNF1A, HNF4A, GCK most common; genetic testing confirms
    \item \textbf{Secondary diabetes:} pancreatic (cystic fibrosis, chronic pancreatitis, haemochromatosis, pancreatic cancer), drug-induced (steroids, antipsychotics, checkpoint inhibitors), endocrine (Cushing, acromegaly)
    \item \textbf{Ketosis-prone T2D (Flatbush):} DKA at presentation, obesity, autoantibody negative, $\beta$-cell recovery; predominantly African/Caribbean ancestry
    \item \textbf{Type 1b (idiopathic):} no autoantibodies, permanent insulin dependence; rare, mostly non-European ancestry
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Urgent assessment for: polyuria/polydipsia/weight loss in child/adult; suspected DKA (vomiting, abdominal pain, Kussmaul breathing, drowsiness). Do not delay -- DKA is life-threatening.

\textbf{Contact your local emergency services for:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item DKA: decreased consciousness, severe dehydration, hypotension, Kussmaul breathing
    \item Severe hypoglycaemia: seizures, unconsciousness, unable to swallow
    \item Pump failure with ketosis (if on insulin pump)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis and Investigations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Diagnostic criteria (any one):} fasting glucose $\ge$7.0 mmol/L, random glucose $\ge$11.1 mmol/L with symptoms, HbA1c $\ge$6.5\% (48 mmol/mol), OGTT 2-h $\ge$11.1 mmol/L; confirm with repeat unless unequivocal symptoms + random glucose $\ge$11.1
    \item \textbf{Autoantibodies:} GAD65, IA-2, ZnT8, insulin (IAA -- only if not on insulin); $\ge$2 positive confirms autoimmune aetiology
    \item \textbf{C-peptide:} low/undetectable at diagnosis (except honeymoon); distinguishes from T2D/MODY; measured fasting or post-stimulation (mixed meal)
    \item \textbf{Ketones:} blood $\beta$-hydroxybutyrate (preferred) or urine acetoacetate; essential at diagnosis and during illness
    \item \textbf{Baseline screening:} thyroid function (TSH, TPO antibodies), coeliac serology (tTG-IgA + total IgA), lipids, renal function, LFTs, urinary ACR, retinal photography, BP, height/weight/BMI
    \item \textbf{Genetic testing:} if MODY suspected (young, lean, family history, negative antibodies, measurable C-peptide)
    \item \textbf{Technology setup:} CGM (continuous glucose monitoring) -- rtCGM (Dexcom G6/G7, Guardian 4) or isCGM (FreeStyle Libre 2/3); insulin pump eligibility assessment
\end{itemize}

\section*{Management}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Insulin therapy (lifelong, essential):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Multiple daily injections (MDI):} basal-bolus regimen; long-acting basal (glargine U100/U300, degludec, detemir) once/twice daily + rapid-acting bolus (aspart, lispro, glulisine, faster-aspart, faster-lispro) with meals; dose adjustment by carbohydrate counting, correction factors, insulin-to-carb ratios
        \item \textbf{Insulin pump therapy (CSII):} continuous rapid-acting basal + bolus; advanced hybrid closed-loop systems (MiniMed 780G, Tandem t:slim X2 with Control-IQ, Omnipod 5, CamAPS FX) automate basal delivery based on CGM; gold standard for many
        \item \textbf{Concentrated insulins:} glargine U300, degludec U200, lispro U200 for high doses
        \item \textbf{Adjuncts (off-label/emerging):} SGLT2 inhibitors (reduce insulin dose, weight, HbA1c; DKA risk -- requires ketone monitoring); GLP-1 RAs (weight, insulin dose reduction); metformin (insulin resistance); pramlintide (amylin analogue, US only)
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Glucose targets (ISPAD/ADA):} HbA1c $<$7.0\% (53 mmol/mol) for most; $<$6.5\% (48) if achievable without hypoglycaemia; $<$7.5\% (58) for young children/high hypoglycaemia risk; individualise; TIR (Time in Range 3.9--10.0 mmol/L) $>$70\%, TBR $<$4\%, TAR $<$25\%
    \item \textbf{Carbohydrate counting:} essential skill; dietitian-led education; apps/bolus calculators
    \item \textbf{Sick-day rules:} never stop insulin; increase monitoring (glucose + ketones 2--4 hourly); supplemental rapid insulin per correction scale; hydrate (oral/IV); seek care if ketones rising, vomiting, unable to maintain fluids
    \item \textbf{Hypoglycaemia management:} 15 g fast-acting carb (glucose tabs, juice, jellybeans) if conscious; recheck 15 min; glucagon (IM/SC/nasal) if unconscious/seizure; mini-dose glucagon for mild recurrent hypoglycaemia
    \item \textbf{Exercise management:} reduce basal/bolus pre-exercise; carb intake during; monitor for delayed hypoglycaemia (6--24 h post); CGM alerts essential
    \item \textbf{Technology:} CGM for all (covered in some public systems for T1D); hybrid closed-loop pumps (public medicines subsidy for eligible); smart pens (InPen, NovoPen 6); data sharing (Clarity, Glooko, Diasend, Tidepool)
    \item \textbf{Multidisciplinary team:} endocrinologist/diabetologist, diabetes nurse educator, dietitian, psychologist, podiatrist, ophthalmologist, exercise physiologist, social worker
    \item \textbf{Complication screening (annual):} retinal photography, urinary ACR, eGFR, lipids, BP, foot exam, thyroid (T1D), coeliac (if symptomatic), psychosocial (diabetes distress, depression, eating disorders)
    \item \textbf{Immunisation:} influenza, pneumococcal, COVID-19, HPV; no contraindication to live vaccines
    \item \textbf{School/work:} diabetes management plan, hypo kit, staff training, exam provisions, driver licensing (conditional, medical review)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Complications}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Acute:} DKA (mortality <1\% in experienced centres; cerebral oedema rare but fatal in children), severe hypoglycaemia (seizures, injury, cognitive impact, fear), HHS (rare in T1D)
    \item \textbf{Chronic microvascular:} retinopathy (leading cause of blindness in working age), nephropathy (albuminuria $\to$ ESRD), neuropathy (distal symmetric, autonomic -- gastroparesis, erectile dysfunction, cardiac autonomic neuropathy)
    \item \textbf{Chronic macrovascular:} CVD (2--4x risk), stroke, PAD; risk accelerated by hyperglycaemia, hypertension, dyslipidaemia
    \item \textbf{Other:} frozen shoulder, limited joint mobility, Dupuytren's, necrobiosis lipoidica, lipohypertrophy (rotation!), eating disorders (diabulimia -- insulin omission for weight loss), diabetes distress/burnout (30--40\%), depression/anxiety (2x general population)
    \item \textbf{Pregnancy:} preconception HbA1c $<$6.5\% (48); tight targets (fasting $<$5.1, 1-h post $<$7.0, 2-h $<$6.7); technology strongly recommended; folic acid 5 mg; specialist multidisciplinary care
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prognosis and Outcomes}
Life expectancy has improved dramatically: DCCT/EDIC showed intensive control reduces complications by 35--76\% and extends life. Modern technology (CGM, hybrid closed-loop) achieves TIR >70\% in many, with HbA1c <7\% without severe hypoglycaemia. Median life expectancy gap vs general population now ~8--10 years (was ~20 years); near-normal with optimal care. Complication rates declining in cohorts diagnosed after 1990. Research towards disease modification: teplizumab (anti-CD3) delays Stage 3 onset by ~2 years (FDA approved for Stage 2); antigen-specific immunotherapy, $\beta$-cell replacement (stem cell-derived islets, encapsulation), artificial pancreas refinement.

\section*{Prevention and Self-Care}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{No proven primary prevention yet:} teplizumab for Stage 2 (high-risk relatives); trial participation (ENDIA, INTR1D, oral insulin, abatacept, JAK inhibitors)
    \item \textbf{Secondary prevention (complications):} glucose targets, BP $<$130/80, statin >40 yr or CVD risk factors, ACEi/ARB if albuminuria, SGLT2i for cardiorenal protection (with DKA mitigation), annual screening
    \item \textbf{Daily self-management:} CGM review, bolus for all carbs, site rotation, pump/cannula changes q3d, sick-day preparedness (ketone strips, glucagon, rapid insulin, electrolytes)
    \item \textbf{Psychosocial:} diabetes distress screening (PAID, DDS); psychologist referral; peer support (JDRF, a local diabetes organisation, T1D meetups, camps for kids)
    \item \textbf{Technology optimisation:} review CGM/pump data weekly; adjust basals, ratios, targets; use automated features
    \item \textbf{School/work advocacy:} know rights (Disability Discrimination Act); management plans; exam adjustments
    \item \textbf{Driving:} check glucose before and 2-hourly on long trips; hypo kit in car; notify licensing authority
    \item \textbf{Support organisations:} a local diabetes organisation, a local diabetes organisation, Australasian Diabetes Educators Association, local Diabetes Society, Type 1 Diabetes Network
\end{itemize}
