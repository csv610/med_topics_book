\chapter{Cholesterol and lipid tests (blood test)}

\section*{Overview}
Cholesterol and lipid tests (blood test) is a test used to help find out how the body is working, to look for a particular condition, or to monitor a known problem. Tests may involve taking a sample (such as blood, urine, or tissue) or scanning the body from the outside. Some tests are part of routine screening for healthy people; others are done when a person has symptoms.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Most tests cause little or no discomfort
    \item A small bruise or sting from a needle is common after a blood test
    \item Some scans involve an injection of contrast dye or lying still inside a scanner
    \item Mild anxiety before or during the test is common
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A doctor may order a test to confirm or rule out a diagnosis
    \item Screening tests are offered to healthy people at certain ages or risk levels
    \item Tests are repeated to follow a known condition or treatment response
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Test selection is based on the symptom or question to be answered
    \item Some tests need preparation such as fasting or a full bladder
    \item Samples are analysed in a laboratory; scans are read by a radiologist
    \item Results are interpreted together with the person's symptoms and history
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A test itself is not a treatment, but results guide treatment decisions
    \item Follow-up may include more tests, referral to a specialist, or starting therapy
    \item Discuss what the results mean and what happens next with the doctor
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Take part in screening programs recommended for your age and sex
    \item Ask your doctor what a test involves and how to prepare
    \item Tell the doctor about allergies, pregnancy, or previous problems with a test
    \item Keep a record of results and follow-up plans
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
\begin{itemize}
    \item If you have symptoms that have not been explained, ask the doctor whether a test is appropriate
    \item If you feel faint, unwell, or have a reaction during a test, tell the staff immediately
    \item If results are abnormal, follow up to discuss the next steps
\end{itemize}
