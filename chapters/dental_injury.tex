\chapter{Dental injury}

\section*{Overview}
Dental injury is an injury that can affect the skin, muscles, bones, joints, or other tissues. Injuries may result from accidents, falls, sports, motor vehicle crashes, bites, or overuse. Most minor injuries recover with simple first aid and rest, while more serious injuries need medical assessment and sometimes surgery or rehabilitation.

\section*{Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Pain or tenderness at the site
    \item Swelling, bruising, or redness
    \item Loss of movement or strength
    \item Deformity, or a limb that looks out of place
    \item Numbness, tingling, or weakness
    \item Bleeding, cuts, or grazes
    \item Inability to bear weight or use the part normally
\end{itemize}

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Falls, trips, and slips
    \item Sports and recreational activities
    \item Motor vehicle and bicycle accidents
    \item Bites, stings, and blows
    \item Repetitive or overuse movements
    \item Hot surfaces, chemicals, or electricity (for burns)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis}
\begin{itemize}
    \item History of how the injury happened and physical examination
    \item X-ray to check for broken bones
    \item Ultrasound, CT, or MRI for soft-tissue, joint, or internal injuries
    \item Checking blood flow, sensation, and movement below the injury
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item First aid using the RICE approach for many soft-tissue injuries: Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation
    \item Cleaning and dressing wounds, and tetanus cover if needed
    \item Pain relief with paracetamol or anti-inflammatories
    \item Splints, slings, plasters, or braces to protect the part
    \item Stitches, glue, or tape for cuts
    \item Physiotherapy and gradual return to activity
    \item Surgery for severe injuries such as fractures that are out of place or damaged internal organs
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prevention}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Wear seatbelts, helmets, and recommended protective gear
    \item Warm up and cool down when exercising
    \item Keep walkways, stairs, and lighting safe at home
    \item Use the right technique and equipment for sport and work
    \item Take breaks from repetitive tasks
    \item Keep physical fitness and strength well maintained
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
\textbf{Call 000} for major trauma, heavy bleeding, suspected broken bones that look out of place, head injury with confusion or loss of consciousness, or any injury that affects breathing. Otherwise seek care if:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Pain and swelling are severe or worsening
    \item The limb looks deformed or cannot be used
    \item There is numbness, tingling, coldness, or colour change below the injury
    \item A wound is deep, dirty, or has something embedded in it
    \item Symptoms do not improve after a few days of self-care
\end{itemize}
