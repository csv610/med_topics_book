\chapter{MS-2 Step (medical abortion medicine)}
\index{MS-2 Step (medical abortion medicine)}

\section*{Definition and Overview}
MS-2 Step is a composite pharmaceutical pack registered in many countries for the medical termination of an active intrauterine pregnancy of up to 63 days (9 weeks) gestation. The pack contains two distinct medications: one tablet of mifepristone (200 mg) and four tablets of misoprostol (200 mcg each, totalling 800 mcg). Mifepristone acts as a selective progesterone receptor antagonist, blocking the hormone necessary to maintain pregnancy. Misoprostol is a synthetic prostaglandin E1 analogue that induces uterine contractions and cervical ripening to expel the conceptus. MS-2 Step is a crucial component of reproductive healthcare, offering a non-invasive, highly effective alternative to surgical abortion under appropriate clinical supervision.

\section*{Epidemiology}
In many countries, medical termination of pregnancy (MTOP) using MS-2 Step has become the predominant method for early abortion since its listing on the public medicines subsidy (public medicines subsidy) in 2013. Approximately 60\% to 70\% of early terminations are now conducted medically rather than surgically. Access to the medication is regulated, requiring prescribing clinicians (GPs and obstetricians) to undergo specific certification. The utilization of MTOP has significantly improved access to reproductive healthcare for women in rural, regional, and remote areas of some countries, where access to surgical facilities is limited. The success rate of MS-2 Step is high, with approximately 95\% to 98\% of patients achieving complete termination without requiring surgical intervention.

\section*{Aetiology and Pathophysiology}
The mechanism of action of MS-2 Step involves a sequential two-stage pharmacological process that mimics the physiological process of spontaneous miscarriage.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mifepristone mechanism:} Mifepristone binds with high affinity to progesterone receptors, competitively inhibiting endogenous progesterone. This leads to endometrial degeneration, decidual necrosis, cervical softening, and detachment of the blastocyst.
    \item \textbf{Sensitisation of the myometrium:} Mifepristone increases the sensitivity of the myometrium to prostaglandins, magnifying the contractile effects of the subsequently administered misoprostol.
    \item \textbf{Misoprostol mechanism:} Administered 36 to 48 hours after mifepristone, misoprostol binds to prostaglandin receptors in the myometrium, causing strong uterine contractions and cervical dilation, leading to the expulsion of the gestational tissue.
    \item \textbf{Contraindications:} Contraindicated in patients with confirmed or suspected ectopic pregnancy, chronic adrenal failure, severe unmanaged asthma, known hypersensitivity to the components, or an intrauterine device (IUD) in situ (which must be removed prior to administration).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Clinical Features}
The clinical progression of MTOP involves predictable symptoms that correspond to the physiological expulsion of pregnancy tissue.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Mild or absent symptoms following the administration of mifepristone (some patients may experience light vaginal bleeding).
    \item Moderate-to-severe abdominal cramping and uterine pain commencing within 1 to 4 hours after taking misoprostol.
    \item Vaginal bleeding, typically heavier than a normal menstrual period, containing clots and visible gestational tissue, usually peaking 2 to 6 hours after misoprostol.
    \item Systemic side effects of misoprostol, including transient fever, chills, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, headache, and dizziness.
    \item Gradual subsidence of severe pain and heavy bleeding over 24 to 48 hours, transitioning to light bleeding or spotting that may persist for up to 2 weeks.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Differential Diagnosis}
In patients presenting with pain or bleeding during or after MS-2 Step therapy, alternative conditions must be considered.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ectopic pregnancy:} A life-threatening condition where the embryo implants outside the uterine cavity; must be ruled out via pre-treatment ultrasound, as MS-2 Step is ineffective for ectopic pregnancies.
    \item \textbf{Incomplete abortion:} Retention of gestational products resulting in persistent heavy bleeding and pain; requires clinical evaluation and potentially surgical vacuum aspiration or additional misoprostol.
    \item \textbf{Septic abortion:} Intrauterine infection complicating the abortion process; presents with fever, pelvic pain, purulent discharge, and systemic toxicity.
    \item \textbf{Undiagnosed molar pregnancy:} Abnormal gestational trophoblastic disease; presents with exceptionally high hCG levels and characteristic ultrasound findings.
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to Seek Medical Care}
Patients undergoing medical termination must be fully informed of the expected clinical course and have access to 24-hour medical support.

\textbf{Contact your local emergency services or attend the nearest emergency department for:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Extremely heavy bleeding, defined as soaking through two or more maximal-use sanitary pads per hour for two consecutive hours
    \item Severe, intolerable abdominal pain that is unresponsive to prescribed analgesics
    \item Fever of $38^\circ$C or higher that persists for more than 4 hours, or starts more than 24 hours after misoprostol administration
    \item Foul-smelling vaginal discharge
    \item Signs of shock, such as severe dizziness, fainting, rapid heart rate, or confusion
\end{itemize}

\section*{Diagnosis and Investigations}
Clinical protocol mandates specific investigations before and after administering MS-2 Step to ensure safety and confirm completion.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pre-treatment pelvic ultrasound:} The gold standard to confirm intrauterine pregnancy and accurately document gestational age (must be $\le 63$ days).
    \item \textbf{Rh factor screening:} Performed prior to treatment; Rh-negative women require administration of anti-D immunoglobulin within 72 hours of misoprostol administration to prevent isoimmunisation.
    \item \textbf{Baseline serum beta-hCG:} Measured pre-treatment if follow-up is to be conducted via blood tests.
    \item \textbf{Post-treatment success confirmation:} Performed 14 to 21 days after mifepristone. This is done using a low-sensitivity urine hCG test (supplied with the pack) or by confirming a drop of $\ge 80$\% in serum beta-hCG compared to baseline. A follow-up ultrasound may also be used.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Management}
The clinical protocol for administering MS-2 Step is highly structured and must be followed carefully.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1 (Mifepristone):} One 200 mg tablet taken orally with water on Day 1 under the direction of the certified prescriber.
    \item \textbf{Step 2 (Misoprostol):} Four 200 mcg tablets (800 mcg total) administered buccally (placed in the cheek pouches) 36 to 48 hours after Step 1. The tablets must be kept in the cheeks for 30 minutes, after which any remaining fragments may be swallowed with water.
    \item \textbf{Analgesia management:} Proactive pain relief is essential. Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen are first-line, as they directly target prostaglandin-mediated uterine spasms. Co-codamol or low-dose oral morphine may be prescribed for severe pain.
    \item \textbf{Antiemetic therapy:} Metoclopramide or prochlorperazine may be administered to prevent or manage nausea.
    \item \textbf{Follow-up consultation:} Mandated at 2 weeks to review clinical symptoms, confirm complete abortion, and discuss future contraception.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Complications}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Incomplete abortion or retained products of conception (RPOC), occurring in 2\% to 5\% of cases and potentially requiring surgical curettage.
    \item Severe haemorrhage requiring blood transfusion (less than 1\% of cases).
    \item Intrauterine infection or endometritis, which can progress to life-threatening sepsis (including toxic shock syndrome caused by \textit{Clostridium sordellii}).
    \item Continued viable pregnancy, requiring surgical termination to prevent potential fetal malformations associated with misoprostol exposure.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Prognosis and Outcomes}
The prognosis for patients undergoing MTOP with MS-2 Step is excellent. The treatment is highly successful, with complete termination achieved in over 95\% of cases. Normal menstruation typically resumes within 4 to 6 weeks following the procedure. Future fertility is not adversely affected by uncomplicated medical termination. Long-term psychological outcomes are generally favourable, particularly when patients receive non-directive, supportive counselling before and after the procedure.

\section*{Prevention and Self-Care}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Rest in a comfortable, private environment on the day of misoprostol administration, with access to a toilet and support from a trusted companion.
    \item Use high-absorbency sanitary pads rather than tampons or menstrual cups during the abortion process to minimize the risk of infection.
    \item Avoid strenuous physical activity, heavy lifting, or sexual intercourse until bleeding has subsided.
    \item Discuss and initiate a suitable method of contraception immediately, as ovulation can occur as early as 8 days after medical termination.
    \item Complete the mandatory follow-up evaluation at 2 weeks to confirm the pregnancy has been fully resolved.
\end{itemize}
