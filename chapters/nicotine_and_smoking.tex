\chapter{Nicotine and smoking}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Educational draft; seek professional advice for individual care.}

\section*{Definition and scope}
Nicotine and smoking focuses on prevention, health behaviour, exposure reduction, or practical safety. Advice should be adapted to age, disability, pregnancy, and medical risk.

\section*{Contributors and risk}
Risk is shaped by environment, occupation, housing, access to care, habits, medicines, chronic disease, and social circumstances.

\section*{Presentation}
Identify the exposure or goal, current practices, barriers, warning symptoms, and vulnerable people.

\section*{Assessment}
Clinicians can assess baseline health, immunisation and screening needs, medication interactions, nutrition, and environmental risk.

\section*{Management}
Useful measures include preparation, protective equipment, safer routines, vaccination, hydration, gradual activity, and support services.

\section*{When to seek urgent care}
Get urgent help for exposure-related breathing difficulty, collapse, confusion, suspected poisoning, serious injury, or rapid deterioration.

\section*{Prevention and follow-up}
Review the plan regularly and use current local public-health and emergency guidance.

\section*{Sources and limitations}
This concise educational overview should be checked against current local clinical guidance and adapted to the individual.
