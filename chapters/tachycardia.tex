\chapter{Tachycardia}

\section{Overview}
Tachycardia is a heart rate exceeding 100 beats per minute in adults. While some tachycardia is normal during exercise or stress, pathological tachycardia can indicate underlying heart conditions or other medical problems. Types include sinus tachycardia, atrial tachycardia, atrial flutter, atrial fibrillation, ventricular tachycardia, and supraventricular tachycardia (SVT).

\section{Symptoms}
Common symptoms include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Palpitations (feeling of rapid or pounding heartbeat)
    \item Dizziness or lightheadedness
    \item Shortness of breath
    \item Chest pain or discomfort
    \item Fatigue and weakness
    \item Fainting or near-fainting (syncope)
    \item Anxiety
\end{itemize}

Ventricular tachycardia can be life-threatening and may cause sudden cardiac arrest.

\section{Causes}
Tachycardia causes vary by type:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sinus tachycardia:} Exercise, fever, anxiety, dehydration, anemia, hyperthyroidism, heart failure
    \item \textbf{SVT:} Abnormal electrical pathways in the heart
    \item \textbf{Atrial fibrillation:} High blood pressure, heart disease, thyroid problems, alcohol
    \item \textbf{Ventricular tachycardia:} Coronary artery disease, heart attack, cardiomyopathy
    \item \textbf{Medications:} Stimulants, decongestants, thyroid medication
\end{itemize}

\section{Diagnosis}
Diagnostic evaluation includes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ECG/EKG:} Records electrical activity to identify rhythm
    \item \textbf{Holter monitor:} 24-48 hour continuous monitoring
    \item \textbf{Event recorder:} Portable device for intermittent symptoms
    \item \textbf{Blood tests:} Thyroid function, electrolytes, cardiac enzymes
    \item \textbf{Echocardiogram:} Ultrasound to assess heart structure
    \item \textbf{Stress test:} Heart monitoring during exercise
\end{itemize}

\section{Treatment}
Treatment depends on type and cause:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Vagal maneuvers:} Deep breathing, bearing down, carotid massage
    \item \textbf{Medications:} Beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers, antiarrhythmics
    \item \textbf{Cardioversion:} Electrical shock to restore normal rhythm
    \item \textbf{Catheter ablation:} Destroy abnormal electrical pathways
    \item \textbf{Implantable devices:} Pacemaker or ICD for recurrent cases
    \item \textbf{Treat underlying cause:} Correct anemia, thyroid disorders, etc.
\end{itemize}

\section{Prevention}
Prevention strategies include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Managing stress and anxiety
    \item Limiting caffeine and alcohol
    \item Quitting smoking
    \item Regular exercise
    \item Maintaining healthy weight
    \item Controlling blood pressure and cholesterol
    \item Managing underlying conditions
\end{itemize}

\section{When to Seek Medical Care}
Seek emergency care for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chest pain or pressure
    \item Fainting or loss of consciousness
    \item Severe shortness of breath
    \item Heart rate over 150 at rest
    \item Symptoms lasting more than a few minutes
\end{itemize}
