\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This book is an educational draft of medical topics for healthcare students, clinicians, and informed patients. The content is AI-assisted and drawn from general medical knowledge. Individual chapters do not yet carry source citations, and every chapter is marked as under review. Content should be treated with caution and confirmed with a qualified healthcare provider before relying on it.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{How to Use This Book}

This book is intended as a reference, not a diagnostic tool. If you are experiencing symptoms, look them up by name and read the relevant chapter to understand possible causes and what steps to take. The ``When to Seek Medical Care'' section in each chapter provides clear guidance on when professional evaluation is needed.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Important Disclaimer}

The information in this book is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in this book.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately.
